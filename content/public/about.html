About MyTarot.Day

MyTarot.Day offers a free daily tarot reading, drawn entirely by you. Each visit, a unique four-card spread is created with a Determinant, Past, Present, and Future perspective — offering a moment of reflection, curiosity, and personal insight.

How the reading works

The deck shuffles and opens into a fan of face-down cards right on the page. You click four of them yourself, and once you've picked your four, they turn over to reveal your reading — no birth date, no personal number, no sign-up. Every draw is genuinely random, so no two readings are ever exactly alike.

MyTarot.Day is designed to connect the symbolism of the tarot tradition with a modern digital experience, allowing anyone around the world to explore these ancient symbols in a simple and accessible way.

Created by P.San Team

MyTarot.Day was created by P.San Team, a small independent team dedicated to building digital experiences that bring traditional knowledge, creativity, and technology together.

Our goal is to make symbolic and reflective experiences available to everyone, with simple access, no registration required, and respect for the traditions that inspired them.

The artwork

Card illustrations are from the original 1909 Rider–Waite–Smith tarot deck, illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith. This classic deck is one of the most influential tarot traditions and continues to inspire readers around the world.

What this is — and isn't

MyTarot.Day is offered for reflection and entertainment. Tarot has a long history as a symbolic and reflective practice, but it isn't a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice. Use it as a moment for personal reflection, not as a directive.
